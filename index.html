Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I have my home or driveway pressure washed in Miami?
Due to Miami's humid climate and coastal conditions, we recommend pressure washing exterior surfaces at least once or twice a year to prevent mold, algae, and salt air buildup.
What is the difference between pressure washing and soft washing?
Pressure washing uses high-pressure water streams to deep clean hard surfaces like concrete driveways. Soft washing uses low pressure combined with specialized safe cleaning solutions to gently sanitize roofs, stucco, and delicate siding without damage.
Will pressure washing damage my roof shingles or windows?
No. For delicate surfaces like roof tiles and windows, we utilize specialized low-pressure soft washing techniques that eliminate dirt and stains while preserving all materials and seals.
Do I need to be home during the exterior cleaning service?
You do not need to be home during the service as long as exterior water spigots are accessible and all doors and windows remain securely closed.
Are your cleaning solutions safe for pets and surrounding landscape?
Yes, we use eco-friendly, biodegradable cleaning formulations. Our team also pre-wets surrounding plants and foliage before and after cleaning to ensure safe, complete protection.
